// 01 START HERE

Quick Start

You join a dark horror map. Every round, one player in every four becomes a Ghost - invisible, silent and hungry. Everyone else is an Investigator with a flashlight, a laser and a few tricks. Find the ghosts before they find you.

  1. Check your side. The top-left of your screen says GHOST or INVESTIGATOR. Roles rotate every round, so everyone gets a turn as a Ghost.
  2. Use the menu. Press M (Choose Team) or say /menu; B opens the weapons shop. A menu never opens by itself and closes after 20 seconds.
  3. Ghost? You are invisible. Follow the PREY line on your HUD and hunt the humans.
  4. Investigator? Press F for your flashlight. A green laser means nothing is there; a red laser means a Ghost is in your sights - fire!
  5. Win by banishing every ghost, surviving until dawn, lighting all three candles, or shattering the Ghost Anchor. Ghosts win by killing every Investigator.
  6. Level up to unlock Ghost classes, weapons, roles and perks. Say /howto for the in-game guide.
No downloads

Everything runs on the server. You do not need to install anything - no models, sounds or maps.

// 02 THE MOD

What is Hidden Haunted Seek?

Hidden Haunted Seek (HHS) is a horror hide-and-seek game mode for Counter-Strike 1.6, written as an AMX Mod X plugin by Néć[Ř]ø /A/. It is designed for dark, atmospheric horror maps and for roughly 8 to 16 players.

One side is hidden - completely invisible and silent. The other side is armed, but afraid: it is dark, their nerves are failing, and the thing they are hunting can be standing right behind them.

// 03 THE FLOW

How a Round Works

  • Round startRoles are rotated automatically (one Ghost per 4 players - whoever was a Ghost longest ago goes first). Press M (Choose Team) to open the menu.
  • Freeze time, then the huntA banner tells you your role, and special nights (a rare moon or a modifier) are announced. The dawn timer starts counting down from 4:00.
  • Shortly after the hunt beginsInvestigators get a buddy. With 6 or more players, an objective appears: three candles or a Ghost Anchor.
  • Every 60 secondsA respawn wave brings fallen players back (up to 3 waves, never in the last 20 seconds before dawn).
  • Between 1 and 2.5 minutes inTrick or Treat: one random event for everyone.
  • ThroughoutThunder, lights flickering, soul orbs, infection and Last Rites, abilities and skills - see the sections below.
  • Last 90 secondsThe last minute: the timer turns red - hold on until dawn.
  • Dawn - or earlierWhen the timer hits zero the ghosts are banished and the Investigators win - unless the round ended sooner. A recap shows the MVP and the leaders, then roles rotate and the next round begins.
// 04 THE GOAL

How to Win

Investigators win by...

  • Banishing every Ghost (kill them all)
  • Surviving until dawn - the default is 4:00
  • Lighting all 3 candles at once
  • Shattering the Ghost Anchor

Ghosts win by...

  • Killing every Investigator
  • A victim you kill rises as a Ghost after a few seconds - unless the team saves them with the Last Rites, so your side grows as the round goes on
Good to know

Candles and the anchor only appear with at least 6 players, and a round uses one or the other (the anchor in about 3 rounds out of 10). Respawn waves bring fallen players back during the round, so a round is rarely over after the first deaths.

// 05 THE GHOSTS

Playing a Ghost

You are the thing in the dark. Nobody can see you, nobody can hear you - until you make a mistake.

The basics

  • Invisible and silent, but fragile: only 150 HP. You leave no body when you die.
  • You fight with a knife that hits about 3 times harder than a normal one. You can't buy anything or pick up weapons.
  • Night vision: a slight glow around you that only you see. F toggles your private coloured flashlight - only other Ghosts can see it.
  • Other Ghosts appear to you as a coloured aura (you choose your own aura colour).
  • Soul Drain: every knife hit heals you 15 HP.
  • Your HUD shows PREY - the distance and direction to the nearest Investigator.
  • Infection: every human you kill rises as a Ghost after about 8 seconds, so every kill grows your team.

Energy

Everything a Ghost does costs energy. You have 100 plus 3 per level (Phantoms +20, plus 8 per Deep Reserves rank), it refills at 4 per second, and you gain 35 energy every time you claim a human. Watch the ENERGY bar on your HUD.

You are not perfectly hidden

  • You shimmer into view for half a second when you attack or get hurt.
  • Standing still for ~6 seconds leaves cold mist at your feet.
  • Salt traps, wards, Spirit Scans, marks, Holy Grenades and lightning expose you.
  • EMF detectors beep when you are near, and the lights flicker when you are very close.
  • A laser sight that touches you turns red - even when you are disguised with Mimic.

// 09 THE INVESTIGATORS

Playing an Investigator

You are armed, you have a light - and it is very dark. Stay together, stay calm, watch the laser.

Weapons and money

Press B (or M, then Buy Weapons) to open the Weapons Shop. It lists every gun with its level next to the name, works anywhere on the map for 30 seconds after you spawn, and every gun comes with at least 100 spare rounds (200 for the M249). The old buy menu, auto-buy and the buy-ammo keys are replaced by it. Ammo costs stamina: refill the weapon in your hands (40) or all your weapons (90) at any time, up to 200 spare rounds.

Your tools

  • Flashlight (F) with a battery: it drains 4% per second while on, recharges 2.5% per second while off, and flickers when low. You can pick its colour.
  • Laser sight on every gun: green = clear, red = a Ghost is in your sights.
  • EMF detector: the bar on your HUD shows how close the nearest Ghost is (up to 900 units). Soft beeps fade in only when one is within 500. /sound sets the volume.
  • Health: every Investigator has 500 HP (Thick Skin adds 25) and healing never goes above 500. The normal CS HUD stops at 255 - the mod's HUD shows your real HP.
  • Holy Grenade: your HE grenade also reveals Ghosts caught in the blast and drains their energy.

Sanity

Sanity keeps falling while no teammate is within about 500 units of you, and while your flashlight is off. It recovers when you stay close to teammates with your light on (Priests recover 50% faster), near your buddy, and in Salt Sanctuaries. Below 40% you start to hallucinate - fake footsteps, brief noise, false EMF alarms - and the screen edges get darker. Below 30% your mind starts to cost you health: you lose HP every second, faster the lower your sanity gets (up to 8 HP per second at zero) - by default it stops at 1 HP and never kills you. Ghost abilities like Scare and Fear Aura attack your sanity directly, so stay together and keep your light on.

Stamina

Stamina is the fuel for your skills. You have 800 plus 10 per level (+100 with the Stamina Reserve perk). It regenerates 10 every 5 seconds - 1.5x faster near a teammate (within 600 units), 1.25x in light, 0.6x alone in the dark, and 15% faster under a Full Moon. You also get +50 for banishing a Ghost and +20 for helping light a candle.

// 11 THE INVESTIGATORS

Tools, Roles and Perks

Tools

  • Salt Trap (/trap): hidden salt on the floor. A Ghost that steps on it is revealed for 6 s. 2 per round.
  • Flare (/flare): lights up the dark where you aim for 25 s. 2 per round.
  • Holy Grenade: see above.

Ghost Vision goggles

The old buyable night vision is gone. At level 20 you earn Ghost Vision: night-vision goggles that show every Ghost in range as a green glow. Switch them on with /nv, the N key or the menu.

  • Expensive: every activation costs 300 stamina.
  • They overheat: heat builds while they are on (about 12 seconds from cold). At 100% they shut down and are locked for 45 seconds. A warning appears at 75%.
  • Switching off early keeps the heat - the goggles cool down slowly, and you cannot switch them on again while they are still hot.
  • A Ghost under Shadow Veil stays hidden from the goggles. Range is about 1200 units, and they tint your screen green.
  • The HUD shows GHOST VISION ON, the heat, or the overheat countdown.

// 12 THE INVESTIGATORS

Teamwork

Buddy system

Every Investigator gets a partner at the start of the round (and a new one if theirs dies). Stay within 600 units of your buddy and you gain sanity, stamina and XP. Your HUD shows where your buddy is when they wander off. Two minutes together earns the Inseparable achievement.

Quick Comms

Open /comm and pick a call. Your team sees a coloured beam and a HUD line with the direction and distance; the other team never sees it. Ghosts have their own set.

When you are dead

  • Spirit Whispers (open the menu while dead): warn the teammate you are watching - "Ghost behind you!", "Ghost on your left!" and more.
  • /ping: sense a ghost near the teammate you are spectating (Investigators).
  • Spirit sight: dead Investigators see Ghosts as faint outlines.
  • Vote: fallen players vote on the next night's modifier.
  • Revenge: if a Ghost killed you, the spirit menu offers one revenge per death - your killer is exposed to the whole team for 6 seconds (/revenge).
  • Respawn wave: the HUD counts down to the next one.
// 13 THE NIGHT

Survival Systems

Infection

An Investigator killed by a Ghost rises as a Ghost after about 8 seconds.

Last Rites

Stand next to the body for about 3 seconds (HUD shows progress) and the infection is cancelled: the victim is revived at half health on the spot. Medics and Priests do it 40% faster.

Soul orbs

Fallen Investigators leave a glowing orb for 60 s. Touch it for a random boon: +100 stamina, +40 sanity, a full battery or an extra salt trap.

Respawn waves

Every 60 s (max 3 per round) fallen players return. Investigators come back with 40% of their health.

Last Ghost Rage

When only one Ghost is left, it enrages: faster, and its energy refills twice as quickly.

Trick or Treat

Once a round, something random happens: every Investigator heals, every Ghost gains energy, every Ghost is revealed for 3 s - or the lights go out for 4 s.

Auto rotation

At the end of every round the server rotates the Ghost role: one Ghost per 4 players, chosen from whoever was a Ghost the longest time ago (new players first). You do not need to change team yourself - and please don't (see the rules).

// NEW IN 0.2 - THE NIGHT

Tasks, Events and Streaks

Small goals, big moments and a few scares, so no two rounds feel the same.

Round task

Every round you get one small task, shown top-left under your XP bar: banish ghosts, use skills, stay near your buddy, help light a candle, perform the Last Rites - or, as a Ghost, claim humans, scare them, or use powers. Finish it for +50 XP, ultimate charge and a small refill.

Last Stand

When only one Investigator is left (and the round started with at least three), they get a big moment: +40% health, full stamina, sanity and ultimate meter, endless light for 25 s and a speed burst - and every ghost is exposed for 3 seconds. Hold out until dawn!

Streaks

Banish 2 ghosts in a row = GHOSTBUSTER (+25 XP, stamina), 4 = SPIRIT HUNTER (+60 XP). A Ghost that claims 3 humans in a row is on a KILLING SPREE (+30 XP).

Most Wanted

At 5 claims in a row a Ghost becomes MOST WANTED: its energy is refilled, but it is exposed to every Investigator for 3 seconds every 20 seconds. Banishing it pays a +100 XP bounty.

Poltergeist

Every minute or two the house is haunted: the lights fail, a cold dread costs everyone sanity, whispers crawl out of the walls - or the ghosts surge with extra energy.

Tips and compass

Players under level 6 get a short hint every 40 seconds. Investigators see the direction and distance to the next unlit candle (or the anchor) on their HUD, and every round starts with a one-line GOAL.

// NEW IN 0.4 - THE NIGHT

Thunder at Dawn

When the sun rises or the round ends, lightning strikes 1 or 3 random Investigators: a bolt from the sky, a white flash, a shaking screen and a thunderclap for everyone. It is a show by default (ht_thunder_dmg 0).

// 14 THE NIGHT

Objectives and Haunted Doors

Candle Ritual

  • 3 candles appear, far apart. Investigators see a blue beam over each unlit one.
  • Stand next to an unlit candle for about 5 s to light it.
  • Light all 3 at once and the Investigators win.
  • Ghosts snuff a lit candle by standing on it for about 2.5 s.

Ghost Anchor

  • In some rounds, a purple pillar marks the Anchor instead of candles.
  • Investigators hold their ground beside it for about 18 s to shatter it.
  • Ghosts nearby push the progress back, and Ghosts close to it recharge energy faster.
  • Shatter it and the Investigators win.

Haunted doors

A Ghost can seal the door it is looking at (Haunt Door). A red beam marks it, Investigators can't open it, Ghosts walk right through. It unseals by itself after about 20 seconds - or an Investigator can break it open at once with the Break Door skill. This works on the map's normal doors.

The night is part of the game, and it is pitch dark: the map is always light style a, with a neutral, almost black fog. Your flashlight, flares, candles and the odd lightning flash are the only light. (Server owners can switch this off with ht_pitch 0 to get dimmed-but-visible nights, where the moon changes the darkness.)

The weather and the lights

  • Pitch dark: there is no first light - dawn simply ends the round. With ht_pitch 0 the sky brightens in the last 90 seconds.
  • Thunderstorms: Storm Night has lightning every 6 to 14 seconds; every flash exposes the Ghosts. Normal nights get distant flashes too.
  • Power flickers: the lights flicker now and then - and always when a Ghost is within about 250 units of an Investigator.
  • Fog: Investigators see a dark fog that Ghosts don't (red on a Blood Moon). Fog Bomb and Night Falls thicken it.
  • Changing the light yourself: Investigators have Flares, Flashlight Surge and Light the Sky. Ghosts have Blackout, Light Drain, Fog Bomb and Night Falls.

Halloween preset

An admin switch (ht_halloween 1): double XP, a modifier every round, and pumpkins that appear around the map - touch one for XP and a small boon.

// 16 THE NIGHT

Ultimates

Everyone has an ultimate meter. It fills slowly by itself, and fast from kills (+30), Last Rites (+35), lighting candles (+25), salt traps and wards (+12) and scares (+10). When it hits 100%, say /ult (or bind it).

Dawn Break Investigators

The sky blazes for 18 seconds. Every ghost within about 1100 units of the caster explodes (100 damage and a shove), every ghost is exposed for 6 s, everyone's screen shakes for 10 s, lightning rains around the caster, and every Investigator hits 40% harder for 12 s.

Night Falls Ghosts

For 12 seconds the map is fully dark (light style a) and flashlights die. Investigators lose 20 sanity, hallucinate, shake, and their Ghost Vision goggles overheat and lock. Every Ghost gets Overdrive, is hidden from detectors, refills its energy, resets every cooldown, heals a third of its health and hits 40% harder.

FAQ

Do I have to download anything?

No. Everything runs on the server - there are no custom models, sounds or maps to install.

Why can't I buy a rifle or an AWP?

Every weapon has a level in the shop (B): rifles from 14, snipers from 20, the M249 at 30. Pistols, cheap SMGs and the HE grenade are open from level 1.

How do I buy ammo?

Open the shop (B) and use the Ammo lines: the weapon in your hands costs 40 stamina, all weapons 90. Guns also come with 100+ spare rounds.

Where did the radio go?

The server removes radio commands, voices ("Fire in the hole!") and radio texts. Use Quick Comms (/comm) instead.

Why did I turn into a Ghost?

Either the round's automatic rotation picked you, or a Ghost killed you and you were not saved by the Last Rites in time (about 8 seconds).

How do I save someone from becoming a Ghost?

Walk up to their body and stay next to it for about 3 seconds. The HUD shows the progress. They come back with half health.

How do I open the menu?

Press M (Choose Team) or say /menu. You can also bind a key with bind x menu (or say /autobind).

The menu blocks my weapon keys!

Press 0 to close it. It also closes by itself after 20 seconds.

How do I climb walls as the Nightcrawler?

Pick the Nightcrawler class (level 12). Stand at a wall and HOLD E: you stick to it and crawl where you look. Press jump to leap off. Climbing drains energy.

How does the Grappler hook work?

Unlock the Grappler role (level 24). Aim at something far away and press E: a rope pulls you to that point for up to 5 seconds. It costs 60 stamina plus 20 per second and has a 15 second cooldown. Release E to let go.

What does the red laser mean?

A Ghost is exactly in your line of fire - shoot. A green laser means nothing is there. (A Ghost using Mimic still turns the laser red.)

What is the TASK line under my XP bar?

A small goal for this round (for example banish a ghost or use three skills). Finish it for +50 XP, ultimate charge and a small refill. You get a new one every round.

What is Most Wanted?

A Ghost that claims five humans in a row. It is exposed to every Investigator for 3 seconds every 20 seconds, and banishing it pays a 100 XP bounty.

How do I get night vision?

At level 20 you unlock Ghost Vision goggles. Say /nv or press N. They show Ghosts as a green glow, cost 300 stamina, and overheat after about 12 seconds (then lock for 45 seconds).

Why am I losing health?

Your sanity is below 30%. Low sanity drains health every second. Stand next to your teammates, switch your flashlight on, use Courage or /pray, or stand in a Salt Sanctuary.

The screen is too dark.

That's the point - but flares, your flashlight, Flashlight Surge and Light the Sky help. Moon phases change the darkness each round, and the sky brightens before dawn.

The HUD is too busy / the sounds are too loud.

Use /hud to switch between Clean, Full and Off, and /sound to set the sound volume (or turn the beeps off).

Is my progress saved?

Yes - level, XP, class, colours, talents and settings are saved automatically by your Steam ID (or your name on LAN).

What does that bar next to my HP mean?

The mod draws a bar for each stat. It is full when the stat is full. Your real HP is shown even above 255, which the normal CS HUD cannot do.

How do I go AFK or rejoin?

Say /spec to go spectate. Say /spec again to come back on a random team. /retry or /reconnect rejoins the server.

Can Ghosts see each other?

Yes - other Ghosts show up as a coloured aura, and Ghosts share a private flashlight colour that Investigators never see.

What happens at dawn?

When the countdown ends the ghosts are banished and the Investigators win the round.

Where do I find the full guide in game?

Say /howto for a 19-page guide, /binds for the key bind list and /rules for the rules.

Tips and Strategy

Investigators

  • Stay in pairs. Sanity, stamina regeneration and the Last Rites all like company.
  • Mark, then shoot. Ghosts only have 150 HP - Mark Ghost + Exorcism Round make one fall in a few hits. Call it with Quick Comms.
  • Save the goggles for the right moment. Ghost Vision costs 300 stamina and overheats in about 12 seconds.
  • Wards and salt at doors, Sanctuary in rooms, Flares in big halls.
  • Light the Sky when the dark is winning - and remember it reveals Ghosts for a moment.
  • Never leave a fallen teammate. A Last Rite saves a life (and an infection).
  • Candles need two groups. Split sensibly and light them together.
  • Watch the lights: flickering means a Ghost is very close.

Ghosts

  • Follow PREY or Hunt Mode and hit the lonely, frightened ones.
  • Seal doors, snare corridors, drain the light - then strike.
  • Don't stand still. Cold mist gives you away (Stalkers last longest).
  • Blackout then Blink is deadly; Fear Aura breaks up a group; Overdrive + Night Falls is brutal.
  • Mimic works in the dark - but a red laser will still betray you.
  • Shadow Veil lets you slip past the detector.
  • Protect the candles or the anchor - that is how rounds are lost.

Installation and Settings

This section is for people who run a server. Players can skip it.

Requirements

  • A Counter-Strike 1.6 dedicated server with AMX Mod X 1.8.2 or newer.
  • These AMX Mod X modules enabled in modules.ini: fakemeta, hamsandwich, fun, cstrike, engine (the main loop runs on its server_frame) and nvault (only needed to read saves from older versions).
  • A dark horror map works best, with roughly 8 to 16 players.

Install

  1. Compile horror_hunt.sma with the AMX Mod X compiler and copy the .amxx file to addons/amxmodx/plugins/.
  2. Add a line horror_hunt.amxx to addons/amxmodx/configs/plugins.ini.
  3. Restart the map. Players do not need to download anything.
mp_roundtime 5 // longer than ht_dawn (240 s) so dawn can happen mp_autoteambalance 0 // the mod rotates the teams itself mp_limitteams 0 ht_light_base m // your map's normal brightness letter ht_darkness 55 // how dark the nights are (%)
Lights, lasers and network traffic

Candles, flares, orbs, pumpkins and the anchor are entities, and the laser is an entity beam that starts at the weapon (the look Natsheh's fix gave it), so the server sends far fewer messages. Flashlights use the normal coloured light, one light per tick (ht_flash_double 1 adds a second one). The main logic runs about 10 times a second even though the loop is server_frame; only the laser updates every frame. If the entity laser looks wrong, ht_laser_ent 0 brings back the message-based laser.

If the compiler complains

Two lines near the top of the file can be commented out: #define USE_NVAULT (no saving, no nvault module needed) and #define USE_ROUND_RESPAWN (used by the infection and Last Rites respawn - comment it out if your AMX Mod X says Ham_CS_RoundRespawn is undefined).

The two files: settings and player stats

Both live in addons/amxmodx/configs/.

  • hhs_settings.ini - every setting (all 276 ht_ cvars) in one commented file, grouped into 11 sections. It is read when the map starts, after amxx.cfg, so it overrides it. Change a value and run amx_hhs_reload (admin flag CFG), or just change map.
  • hhs_stats.ini - every saved player on one line: SteamID (or name on LAN), XP, level, kills, class, role, perk, colours... It is rewritten every 5 minutes, at map change and on amx_hhs_stats_save. To promote or demote a player change level= (the XP follows) or xp=; to add a player add a line; to reset one delete the line. Edit it while the server is stopped, or run amx_hhs_stats_save, edit, then amx_hhs_stats_reload.
  • Players from older versions are read from the old vault once and move into hhs_stats.ini the next time they are saved.

Round log

Every round adds one line to logs/hhs_rounds.log: map, night, moon, players, how many ghosts and investigators, the winner (DAWN, CT or GHOSTS), seconds and who was left. It is meant for balancing: after a week you can count which side wins. Switch it off with ht_roundlog 0.

Admin menu

Players with the CFG admin flag can open the admin menu with amx_htmenu or /htadmin: choose the next round's modifier, bring the dawn now, switch infection, automatic rotation and the Halloween preset, change the night darkness, or give yourself XP.
