01 START HERE

Quick Start

Hold jump, strafe with your mouse, and watch the speedometer climb. Kill with the knife, level up, unlock classes and abilities, and spend your money in the shop. That’s BHOP RAGE.

  1. Press M. The hub menu has everything: shop, respawn, classes, skins, tags, stats, races, settings, rules and team change. It also opens when you spawn.
  2. Bind your abilities. Copy the binds below or open Rules, How-to & Best Rates → Key binds.
  3. Bunny hop. Hold SPACE and strafe (A/D + mouse) in the air. Each hop keeps your speed and adds a little more.
  4. Time it for more. Pressing jump the exact moment you land is a perfect hop: the biggest speed gain and bonus XP.
  5. Knife only. Hits in the air hit harder, kills give XP, a speed boost and a combo.
  6. Pick a class and spend Rage Points in the shop - both in the M menu. There is no money and no buy menu.
  7. Race. On maps with zones: leave the start, hit every checkpoint, cross the finish. /restart anytime.
One console tweak

Set cl_forwardspeed 9999; cl_sidespeed 9999; cl_backspeed 9999 once (or M → Settings → Apply bhop move speeds). The default 400 caps class and kill speed bonuses. See Best Rates for smooth hops.

What is BHOP RAGE?

BHOP RAGE is a fast, chaotic, skill-based movement mod for Counter-Strike 1.6, written from scratch for AMX Mod X 1.8.2 as eight small plugins. It mixes bunny hopping, trick jumping, speedrunning and arcade-style knife fights.

CS 1.6AMX Mod X 1.8.2No client downloadv1.3.0Knife only

Movement

  • Auto bhop with skill-based speed gains
  • Combos, perfect hops, long-jump records
  • Dash, double jump, super jump, air dash
  • Launch pads, nitro rings, speed pads
  • Time trials with checkpoints

Rage

  • Knife-only fights, air-hit bonuses, knockback
  • Kill streaks, kill combos, speed boosts
  • 9 classes unlocked by level
  • 15-item shop, Shockwave to Noclip
  • Rank tags, knife skins, leaderboards
03 START HERE

How a Round Works

  • SpawnYou get a knife (and the C4 on bomb maps), your class is applied, and the M menu pops up.
  • First 10 secondsChanging class with /class applies instantly. After that it waits for your next spawn.
  • Hop and fightBuild speed, chain hops, use abilities (they cost energy, which refills by itself).
  • Kills and tricksKnife kills, air kills, streaks, long jumps, speed milestones and tricks all give XP.
  • Rage PointsEvery XP you earn also gives RP. Spend them in the shop (M) - they are saved, and spending never lowers your level.
  • Round endShop limits reset next round. XP, records, class, skin and tag are saved.
04 MOVEMENT

Bunny Hopping

Holding jump makes you hop again the instant you land. Air control is the engine’s own, so real strafing skill still matters.

How to gain speed

  1. Hold SPACE.
  2. In the air, hold A and turn the mouse left smoothly; next hop hold D and turn right.
  3. Don’t hold W while airborne - it kills air acceleration.

Normal CS clamps bhop speed to about 1.2× your run speed. BHOP RAGE removes that clamp and the landing slowdown.

05 MOVEMENT

Combos and Speed Gains

Hop typeHowDefault gain
Perfectpress jump within 0.03 s of landing+6% speed, +2 XP
Goodpress jump yourself within 0.25 s+3% speed
Heldjust holding jump (autobhop)+1% speed

Gains only start above 200 u/s and stop at the server cap (default 3500 u/s, unlimited on bhop_ maps with the insane preset). Staying on the ground longer than 0.25 s resets your combo. Every 10 combo hops pays XP.

Speed milestones

Reaching 500, 750, 1000, 1250, 1500, 2000, 2500 and 3000 u/s pays XP once per map each. Your best speed ever is a personal record and on the leaderboard.

06 MOVEMENT

Jump Stats

After every jump a small panel shows how good it was: strafes, sync (how often your mouse turned the same way as the A/D key you held), pre speed, max speed and the gain. Green = good jump, yellow = okay, red = you lost speed. Aim for 80%+ sync with 4-8 strafes. Toggle with /js.

07 MOVEMENT

Long Jumps

A single jump that lands within 24 units of the take-off height is measured (hull width +32 included). From 230 units it counts: XP, personal best and the server record. Jumps helped by abilities, pads or knockback don’t count.

Stuck after noclip?

Never. When noclip ends inside a wall the mod tries, in order: a free spot nearby → the last free spot you passed while in noclip → a free spawn point → a respawn. Stuck anywhere else? Say /unstuck (free, every 10 s).

12 COMBAT

Rage Points

Rage Points are the server currency. You earn 0.5 RP per XP (a knife kill worth 25 XP gives 12 RP), new players start with 100 RP, and everything is saved on the server with your XP.

  • Spending RP never lowers your XP or level.
  • RP follow the same anti-farm limits as XP (cooldowns and 400 XP per minute).
  • Double XP also doubles the RP you earn while it runs.
  • Money is hidden and buying is disabled on this server.

14 WORLD

Map Cars

Car jump

Jump off a moving car - or press jump while driving one - and you are launched with the car’s full speed plus a forward and upward boost (“CAR JUMP!”, trick XP). Real drivable vehicles always work; trains and props work where the admin enabled them.

Stunt objects

Cars, trucks and buses on maps can be stunt objects. Depending on how the map was built, a car can be a launch surface (land on its roof to be thrown up), a trick (jump over it for XP), a speed zone, a hazard (moving trains hurt) or even a patrolling vehicle.

Why some cars do nothing

A car that is part of the map’s solid geometry isn’t an object the server can move or detect. Admins can still put a launch or trick zone on its roof.

26 COMMUNITY

Rules

Server rules

  1. Respect everyone. No insults, racism, harassment or threats.
  2. No cheating. No aimbot, wallhack, speedhack or external tools.
  3. No exploits. Report map or mod bugs to an admin.
  4. No spam in chat or voice. No advertising.
  5. Listen to the admins. Appeal calmly.

BHOP RAGE rules

  1. Hop scripts are allowed - autobhop is built in, so +jump spam scripts give no advantage.
  2. No strafe hacks or movement cheats (auto-strafers, speedhacks).
  3. No noclip abuse: don’t camp inside walls or under the map with the shop noclip.
  4. No spawn killing right after a respawn.
  5. Races: no shortcuts through map bugs; records with exploits are removed.
27 COMMUNITY

Best Rates and Settings

Smooth bunny hopping needs a stable frame rate and full network updates. Paste into your console (also in game: /rates):

rate 100000 // full bandwidth cl_updaterate 101 // snapshots per second cl_cmdrate 101 // commands per second ex_interp 0.01 // lowest interpolation fps_max 100 // stable bhop physics cl_forwardspeed 9999 // let speed boosts work cl_sidespeed 9999 cl_backspeed 9999 m_rawinput 1 // raw mouse for strafes gl_vsync 0 // no input lag net_graph 3 // watch loss / choke
Loss or choke?

Lower rate to 50000 and cl_updaterate / cl_cmdrate to 60. Keep fps_max 100 - very high FPS changes jump timing in GoldSrc.

28 COMMUNITY

FAQ

Do I have to download anything?

No for the mod itself. Custom knife skins, once added, download automatically from the server.

Why does my speed stop at around 400?

Your client move-speed limits. Run cl_forwardspeed 9999; cl_sidespeed 9999; cl_backspeed 9999.

I hold jump but don’t get faster.

Gains start above 200 u/s and need air strafing: A + mouse left, D + mouse right, no W in the air.

Do I lose my class when I change it?

No. You can switch any time - instantly in the first 10 seconds after spawning, otherwise on your next spawn.

I bought noclip and ended in a wall.

The mod moves you out automatically. If anything else traps you, say /unstuck.

Where do Rage Points come from?

Every XP you earn gives 0.5 RP. They are saved, and spending them never lowers your level.

Why can’t I buy weapons?

This is a knife server: buying and money are disabled. Everything you can get is in the M menu shop.

I jumped off a car and got no boost.

The car has to be a real map entity. Drivable cars always work; for others an admin enables it with /vmenu. Cars that are part of the map’s walls can’t move or boost.

Pressing M doesn’t show the team menu.

M opens the BHOP RAGE hub. Use its last entry, Change Team.

Can I choose an older tag?

Yes - /tag lists every tag you have unlocked, plus Auto and None.

Is my progress saved?

Yes: XP, records, class, knife skin and tag are saved by your SteamID.

Why didn’t my long jump count?

It must land within 24 units of your take-off height, be at least 230 units, and not be helped by abilities or pads.

29 COMMUNITY

Tips and Strategy

  • Learn manual timing. Perfect hops gain 6× more than held hops.
  • Air Dash to turn corners without losing a single unit of speed.
  • Fight from the air. Airborne hits deal ×1.5 - Dash or Super Jump into a fight.
  • Chain kills within 6 s for combo XP and to keep the kill speed boost alive.
  • Buy Double XP before a race - finishes pay 100 XP (and 50 RP, doubled).
  • Spectate good players - the key display shows exactly how they strafe.
  • Drive, then jump at full car speed for a free speed boost into the next bhop chain.
  • Hunt the Most Wanted - one kill can be worth 50+ RP.
  • Jumper + Moon Boots = absurd air time. Vampire + Rage Shield = free healing.
  • Shockwave is best in a crowd, right before a knife dash.

Requirements

  • CS 1.6 dedicated server, Metamod, AMX Mod X 1.8.2 or newer.
  • Modules: fakemeta, hamsandwich, fun, cstrike, nvault.

Install

  1. Copy the 8 .amxx files to addons/amxmodx/plugins/ (or build them with compile.sh / compile.bat).
