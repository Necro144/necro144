GunGame

GunGame [NS] is a Counter-Strike 1.6 gun game mod written from scratch for AMX Mod X: everybody starts with the same pistol, every kill moves you up the weapon ladder, and the first player to win a knife fight on the last level takes the map. The complete guide, from your first Glock kill to the knife.

01 START HERE

Quick Start

Kill with the gun you are given. Every two kills you get the next gun. Get a kill with the knife on the last level and you win the map.

  1. Join a team. You spawn with a Glock-18 and a knife - that's level 1.
  2. Get 2 kills with your gun. You instantly get the next weapon, with a full clip.
  3. Knife someone at any time to steal a level from them.
  4. Don't kill yourself. Falling or your own grenade costs you a level.
  5. Reach the knife level (level 26) and get one knife kill to win.
  6. Press M for your progress, the weapon order, the top 10 winners and the rules.
No buying

There is no money and no buy menu. You can't drop or pick up guns: your level decides your weapon. You respawn right away with 2 seconds of spawn protection.

02 START HERE

What is GunGame?

GunGame is a fast, arcade-style deathmatch for Counter-Strike 1.6. Instead of buying weapons, everybody climbs the same ladder of guns: pistols, shotguns, SMGs, rifles, snipers, the machine gun, a grenade and finally the knife. It rewards aim with every weapon, not just your favourite one.

CS 1.6AMX Mod X 1.8.2No client download26 levelsInstant respawn

Gameplay

  • 26 weapon levels, knife last
  • 2 kills per gun, 1 for grenade and knife
  • Knife steals a level
  • Suicide / fall costs a level
  • Instant respawn + spawn protection

Match

  • 30-second warm-up at map start
  • Late joiners start at the lowest level
  • Leader announcements
  • Winner saved, /ggtop top 10
  • Next map after the win
03 START HERE

How a Match Works

  1. Warm-up (30 s). Everybody plays with the level-1 pistol and kills don't count. When it ends, everybody is reset to level 1 and respawned.
  2. The race. There are no rounds: you respawn 1.5 seconds after dying and keep your level. The HUD on the left shows your level, weapon, kills and the leader.
  3. The knife level. When someone reaches the last level, the whole server is told. Everybody now wants to stop them.
  4. The win. The first knife kill on the last level wins the map. The winner is announced, players freeze, the win is saved and the next map loads 10 seconds later.

06 MATCH

Respawn and Spawn Protection

You respawn 1.5 seconds after dying, with kevlar and a helmet. For the first 2 seconds you glow in your team colour and can't be hurt. Dropped guns vanish right away, so there's nothing to pick up.

07 MATCH

Warm-up and Late Joiners

Each map starts with a 30-second warm-up so everybody can load in: kills don't count and everybody has the level-1 pistol. When the warm-up ends, all levels reset.

If you join in the middle of a match you start at the lowest level anyone is on, so you're not stuck at the bottom while others are far ahead.

08 MATCH

Winning and the Top 10

The winner is announced on screen and in chat, everybody freezes and the next map loads after 10 seconds. Every win is saved by SteamID: say /ggtop to see the top 10 winners of the server.

09 INTERFACE

The M Menu

Press M (the team menu key) or say /gg:

  1. My progress - level, weapon and kills
  2. Weapon order - the ladder with your level marked
  3. Top 10 winners
  4. How to play
  5. HUD on / off
  6. Change team
  7. Admin menu (admins only) - restart GunGame, skip the warm-up, change a player's level
10 INTERFACE

HUD

The yellow panel on the left shows [NS] GunGame, your level and weapon (e.g. Level 11/26: MP5), your kills on this level (Kills: 1/2) and the current leader. During the warm-up it counts down instead. Turn it off in M → HUD.

14 COMMUNITY

FAQ

Do I have to download anything?

No. GunGame runs on the server, and the gg_ maps download automatically from our FastDL. You can also grab the map packs from the FastDL page before you join.

My kill didn't count.

Only kills with your level's weapon count (and knife kills, which steal a level). Team kills and kills during the warm-up never count.

I lost a level - why?

Someone knifed you, or you killed yourself: falling, your own grenade or the kill command all cost a level.

Can I buy or pick up a better gun?

No. There is no money and no buy menu, and dropped guns disappear. Your level decides your weapon.

Is my progress saved?

Your level lasts for the current map. Your wins are saved by SteamID and show in /ggtop.

Why does M not open the team menu?

M opens the GunGame menu once you're in a team. Pick "Change team" in it to get the normal team menu.

15 COMMUNITY

Tips and Strategy

  • Hunt the leader with the knife. Knifing them costs them a level and gives you one.
  • Pistol levels are the fastest. Go for headshots - the Desert Eagle kills in one.
  • Shotguns: get close. Use corners and doorways on the small gg_ maps.
  • Snipers: don't stand still. Everybody respawns right away and comes looking for you.
  • Grenade level: throw, take cover, a new grenade comes every 2 seconds.
  • Knife level: jump and strafe, attack from behind, and stay away from open areas.
16 REFERENCE

For Server Owners

Requires AMX Mod X 1.8.2 with cstrike, fun, fakemeta, hamsandwich and csx (all standard) and ReGameDLL for infinite rounds. No ReAPI or Orpheu. Loads from plugins-gungame.ini; settings in configs/gungame.cfg:

gg_weapon_order "glock18,usp,...,hegrenade,knife" gg_kills_per_level 2 // kills per gun gg_knife_pro 1 // knife kill steals a level gg_suicide_penalty 1 // suicide / fall costs a level gg_respawn_delay 1.5 // seconds gg_spawn_protection 2.0 // seconds, 0 = off gg_warmup 30 // seconds, 0 = off gg_handicap 1 // late joiners start at the lowest level gg_win_delay 10 // seconds before the next map gg_nade_refresh 2.0 // new HE grenade after a throw gg_armor 100 // kevlar + helmet on spawn
Admin flag

d (ban): gg_restart, gg_skipwarmup, gg_setlevel and the admin menu.
