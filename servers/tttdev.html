01 START HERE

Quick Start

Everyone looks the same. A few players are secretly Traitors; the rest are Innocents, led by the Detectives. Traitors win by killing everyone else, Innocents by finding and killing the Traitors.

  1. Check your role. It is shown when the round starts, after a 10 second preparation.
  2. Say /ttt for the command menu with everything in one place.
  3. Innocent? Stay with others, watch who acts strange, and follow the Detective.
  4. Traitor? Blend in, say /buy for traitor gear, and pick your moment.
  5. Detective? You are known to everyone (blue glow). Scan bodies, buy tools with /buy, lead the hunt.
  6. Don't kill without a reason. Killing your own side costs karma and earns warnings.
Dev build

TTT is installed on the server and in testing - it runs when the server is switched to TTT mode (server name [NS] TTT). Numbers on this page are the server's current settings.

02 START HERE

What is TTT?

Trouble in Terrorist Town is a social deduction mod, originally from Garry's Mod, rebuilt for Counter-Strike 1.6 by GuskiS. Nobody knows who the Traitors are - except the Traitors.

CS 1.6AMX Mod X 1.8.2ReAPIv2.5.251 plugins

Innocents and Detectives

  • Find the Traitors by their behavior
  • Identify bodies to learn roles
  • Detectives glow and have their own shop
  • Health Station, Golden Gun, Teleport
  • Win when every Traitor is dead

Traitors

  • Know each other, hidden from everyone else
  • Earn credits for kills
  • C4, Jihad bomb, throwing knife, Silenced USP
  • Fake Detective, Death Station, Newton Launcher
  • Win when everyone else is dead
03 START HERE

How a Round Works

  • Preparation - 10 sEveryone spawns and picks up weapons. Roles are not handed out yet.
  • Roles1 Traitor for every 3 players and 1 Detective for every 6. Traitors see each other; Detectives are revealed to all.
  • The huntTraitors kill quietly, Innocents search for clues, bodies are found and identified.
  • Round endTraitors win when every Innocent and Detective is dead. Innocents win when every Traitor is dead.
  • KarmaSurviving and winning gives karma; killing your own side takes it away.

05 ROLES

Dead Bodies

Players who die leave a body. Walk up to it and press E to identify it: everyone learns who it was and their role, and identified bodies glow in their role's color.

  • On this server anyone can identify a body, not only Detectives.
  • Identifying a body gives +10 karma.
  • A Detective identifying a Traitor's body earns +1 credit.
  • Aim at a player, body or item to see its info (and a player's health).
06 ROLES

Karma and Warnings

Karma is your reputation. You start at 760. It goes up when you play fair and down when you hurt your own side, and it scales the damage you deal - low karma means weaker shots.

EventKarma
Win the round and stay alive+20
Identify a body+10
Damaging your own sideloses karma (0.25 per damage)
Killing your own sideloses a lot (×50 modifier)

Warnings

Killing the wrong people gives warnings (from 5 players): 3 for wrongly killing Innocents, 2 for wrongly killing Traitors or Detectives, 3 in a row overall. Reaching the limit punishes you: karma removed, HP set to 1 and bad aim. Check yours with /tttwarns.

07 SHOP

Credits

Traitors and Detectives get credits to spend in their shop (/buy). Credits only last for the round.

Traitor credits

  • Start with 2
  • +1 every time a quarter of the players are dead
  • +1 for killing a Detective

Detective credits

  • Start with 1
  • +1 every 45 s you survive
  • +1 for killing a Traitor
  • +1 for identifying a Traitor's body

With item sharing you can also buy an item for a teammate instead of yourself.

13 COMMUNITY

FAQ

Do I have to download anything?

No. Maps and TTT models download automatically from our FastDL when you join. You can also get the map packs from the FastDL page.

Why are my shots so weak?

Your karma is low. Karma scales your damage - stop hurting your own side and it climbs back up.

I got HP 1 and terrible aim.

You reached the warning limit for killing the wrong people. Check /tttwarns and play fair.

How do I know who the Traitors are?

You don't - that's the game. Watch behavior, identify bodies and trust the Detective (unless it's a fake).

Why can't I buy anything?

Only Traitors and Detectives have a shop and credits. Innocents use the weapons they find.

Is my progress saved?

Yes - karma, stats and warnings are saved on the server.

14 COMMUNITY

Tips and Strategy

  • Innocents: never wander alone, and remember who was near a body when it was found.
  • Identify every body - roles of the dead are the best clue you will get.
  • Traitors: act normal. Shooting in public gives you away; the Silenced USP and throwing knife do not.
  • Save credits for C4 when the Innocents group up, or the Jihad bomb as a last stand.
  • Detectives: drop a Health Station where your team gathers - and watch who avoids it.
  • A "Detective" acting oddly may be a Traitor with the Fake Detective item.
