Base Builder

Build a fortress, survive the horde. Base Builder is the classic Counter-Strike 1.6 zombie building mod - this is the NS edition (7.5-NS, based on Tirant's Base Builder 7.4) for AMX Mod X. Builders have two and a half minutes to move blocks into a base, then the zombies are released. The complete guide, from your first block to boss rounds.

Quick Start

Builders (CT) build a base out of the map's blocks, then defend it with guns. Zombies (T) break in and infect them. Last team standing wins.

  1. Press M. The main menu has everything: zombie class, shop, weapons, unlocks, upgrades, role, party, colors, stats and voting.
  2. Builders: hold E on a block to grab it during the build phase. Move the mouse to carry it, scroll in and out with your aim, let go to drop it.
  3. Lock your blocks. Aim at a block and say /lock (up to 10) so nobody else can move it.
  4. Pick a role and a gun. /role for a builder perk, and the weapon menu appears when the zombies are released.
  5. Zombies: pick a class with /class and use its ability with G or R.
  6. Earn credits from kills, medals and challenges and spend them on /shop, /unlock and /upgrade. Everything is saved.
Dev build

This is version 7.5-NS, our own fork of Base Builder 7.4. It is installed on the server but still in testing - numbers on this page are the defaults and may change. See Dev Status.

What is Base Builder?

Base Builder is a classic zombie mod for Counter-Strike 1.6. Maps (bb_) are full of movable blocks behind a barrier. The builders turn those blocks into a base while the zombies wait; when the barrier drops, the zombies have to break in.

CS 1.6AMX Mod X 1.8.2bb_ maps onlyv7.5-NS10 plugins

Builders (CT)

  • Grab, move, lock and color blocks
  • 4 roles: Engineer, Medic, Gunner, Scout
  • Party up and share your blocks
  • Repair damaged blocks by holding E
  • 24 guns, most unlocked with credits

Zombies (T)

  • 10 classes, each with an ability
  • Break blocks (2,500 HP each)
  • Infect builders - they join you
  • Shop: Madness, +1000 HP, Speed
  • Upgrades: health, speed, low gravity
03 START HERE

How a Round Works

  • Build phase - 150 sBuilders grab and place blocks. Zombies wait behind the barrier and pick a class.
  • Prep phase - 30 sBlocks can no longer be moved. Builders pick a weapon and take position; everyone votes the best base.
  • ReleaseThe barrier drops and the zombies attack. From now on blocks have health: zombies break them, builders repair them.
  • InfectionA builder killed by a zombie comes back as a zombie after 5 s. Dead zombies respawn after 3 s.
  • Last humanThe last builder alive gets +200 HP and 1.5× damage. Surviving the round pays 50 credits.
  • Round endBuilders win if anyone survives the timer; zombies win when every builder is infected. MVPs are announced and paid.

Every 5th round can be a boss round.

08 ZOMBIES

Playing a Zombie

  • During the build phase, scout the builders' base and pick the class that beats it - Smasher and Chainsaw for thick walls, Jumper and Banshee to get over or around.
  • Abilities are on G (drop) and R (reload). Each class has one.
  • Attack together: Werewolf's Howl and Rejuvinator's Mend help the whole horde.
  • Dying costs you nothing: you respawn after 3 s and still earn 1 credit per death.
  • Shop items for zombies: Madness (5 s invulnerable), +1000 HP and a Speed Boost.

Boss Rounds and Last Human

Boss round

  • Every 5th round (4+ players)
  • One zombie becomes the boss
  • 6,000 HP + 1,500 per builder
  • The builder who kills it gets 100 credits

Last human

  • When one builder is left (4+ players)
  • +200 HP and 1.5× damage
  • Survive the round: 50 credits

Round MVP

At round end the best builder and best zombie are named MVP and get 15 credits each.

19 COMMUNITY

FAQ

Do I have to download anything?

No. Maps, zombie models and sounds download automatically and fast from our FastDL. You can also grab the map packs from the FastDL page before you join.

I can't move a block.

Blocks only move during the build phase, and not if someone else locked it (unless they are in your party). After the release you can only repair.

Is my progress saved?

Yes: credits, unlocked guns and classes, upgrades, medals and challenges are saved by your SteamID.

How do I use my zombie ability?

Press G (drop) or R (reload). The chat tells you when it is on cooldown.

I spawned inside a block / I am stuck.

Say /stuck. You are moved to the nearest free spot. The server also checks every spawn and frees anyone a block is dropped on.

I spawned frozen - the camera is stuck and there is no HUD.

Say /fix. It finishes your join, puts the camera back and resends the HUD. If that does not help, an admin can fix you from M → Admin.

Someone keeps wrecking our base.

Tell an admin: every block move is logged, and admins can build-ban griefers.

Why does M not open the team menu?

M opens the Base Builder menu. Teams are set by the mod: builders are CT, zombies are T.

Tips and Strategy

  • Builders: build small and thick. A tight box with layered walls beats a big base with gaps.
  • Lock your key blocks early - unlocked blocks can be moved by anyone during the build phase.
  • Put an Engineer on repairs and a Medic in the middle; Gunners hold the entrance.
  • Save a Frost Grenade for when the wall breaks.
  • Zombies: attack one wall together - spreading out only helps the builders.
  • Smasher and Chainsaw open walls; Jumper and Banshee get over and through.
  • Spend early credits on upgrades: they last forever.
